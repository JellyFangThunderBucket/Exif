# Privacy Principles

- Local-first by default; no accounts, telemetry, ads, analytics, or remote scripts.
- External AI providers are disabled and not implemented in the MVP.
- Evidence before interpretation: answers cite local artifact IDs and expose confidence and gaps.
- No emotional manipulation: no streaks, feeds, public profiles, or automatic traumatic-memory surfacing.
- User-owned data: export uses portable JSON; deletion and rebuild controls are visible in the vault screen.
